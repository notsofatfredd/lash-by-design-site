Aftercare

Make your set last.

A few simple habits keep your lashes full and fluffy until your infill.

Keep them dry for 24 hours

No water, steam, saunas or sweaty workouts for the first day while the adhesive cures.

Skip oil near your eyes

Oil-based cleansers and makeup removers break down the bond. Use oil-free products.

Brush them every day

A clean spoolie each morning keeps your lashes neat and separated.

No rubbing or picking

Pat your eyes dry and let your lashes shed naturally. Pulling takes natural lashes with them.

Wash them gently

Clean your lashes with a lash-safe foam a few times a week to keep them fresh.

Book your infill on time

Every two to three weeks. We’ll send you a reminder when it’s due.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How long does a full set take?

Classic sets take about two hours, and hybrid and volume sets about two and a half. You’ll see the time for each set when you book.

How should I arrive?

Come with clean lashes and no eye makeup or mascara. Skip caffeine just before if you can, so you can relax for the whole appointment.

How long do extensions last?

Extensions shed with your natural lash cycle. Most clients book an infill every two to three weeks to keep their set full.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, on your first visit. We book it 24 to 48 hours before your set when you book online.
